# Autoresearch consolidation

The local `autoresearch/final-platform` branch is the canonical integration of
all completed research. It descends from all seven Round 1 cycles, all ten
numbered Round 2 milestones, and the Round 3 holistic-volume work.

Detailed records:

- [Technical design](TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md) explains the current architecture
  and its agent-versus-engine boundary.
- [Autoresearch cycle history](AUTORESEARCH_CYCLES.md) records every round,
  cycle, measured change, rejection, and tradeoff.
- [Metrics and regression design](METRICS_AND_REGRESSIONS.md) defines the
  evaluation protocols and explains which numbers can and cannot be compared.

Three older side branches are intentionally not merged: their perspective and
texture changes are patch-equivalent to, or superseded by, commits already in
the final history. Merging them would restore outdated asset palettes.

## Capability progression

- Round 1 established deterministic native compilation, single-image relief,
  mesh ray reconstruction, semantic geometry, calibrated multiview carving,
  clip validation, and cumulative independent grading.
- Round 2 added fixed-camera evidence, harder organic and mechanical anatomy,
  source-guided Minecraft textures, explicit player-wearable export, and
  palette corrections for Chimera and Zombie Devil.
- Round 3 added source-relative anti-pancake gates, actual fitted-component
  preservation, seven-view mesh evidence, portable packaging, and holistic
  semantic benchmarks for the biblical angel and Zetatech Atlus.

## Compact representative gallery

The research archive remains the source of full evidence. These assets cover
the most important edge cases:

- Nian and Tomato Devil: rounded organic semantic volume;
- Militech Chimera: six-legged mechanical anatomy and camouflage;
- Militech Centaur: player-replaceable wearable exoskeleton;
- Biblical angel: radial wings, many eyes, and all-angle inferred anatomy;
- Zetatech Atlus: hard-surface multiview vehicle reconstruction.

In the full research checkout, `benchmarks/FINAL_REPORT.md`,
`benchmarks/round2/STATUS.md`, and `benchmarks/round3/STATUS.md` retain the
detailed gates and grades. The compact production bundle intentionally omits
that media-heavy archive.
