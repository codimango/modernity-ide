These are the assets for ROUND 2 of autoresearch on the img2blockbench platform.
The goal of this is to hone the agentic capabilities of the tool such that flat images of people or subjects are more realistically captured and actual reasoning is involved in the parts and pieces that are needed to be built.

The tools that are already avialable are pretty good, and the first round of autoresearch crossed many benchmarks successfully, especially for the Goldfinch and Nian

We're asking for more visual and reasoning capabilities however. For example when it comes to an image of a character (like gamma) the assumption is that they should not be a flat reflection (pancake style) and that they hold volume and have space.
