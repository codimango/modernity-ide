The final clean-room grader inspected the real-reference comparison sheets,
did not consult prior grade verdicts, reran validation/audits/tests, and passed
all seven assets at a 4.19/5 mean. Its representative four-picture jury chose
Goldfinch, Porsche, Nian, and Gamma and passed all four at 4.21/5.

The system now offers complementary routes rather than pretending that one
image contains unknowable hidden geometry: single-image relief, semantic
authoring, textured-mesh ray voxelization, and calibrated multi-view or clip
visual hulls. Output remains native Blockbench cuboids with bones, pivots,
textures, Bedrock geometry, manifests, and audit evidence.

Known boundary: these are faithful voxel interpretations, not photorealistic
meshes. Hidden concavities and unseen markings require a source mesh or enough
calibrated observed views. Benchmark source media is local-only and subject to
the rights notes in each cycle's provenance record.
